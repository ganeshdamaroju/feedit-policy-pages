Home Privacy Policy Terms

Users can request deletion of their Feedit account and associated profile data.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to:

infotechvvd@gmail.com

Use this subject:

Account deletion request - Feedit

Include the email address used to sign in to the app. If possible, also include your name so we can identify the correct account.

What Data Is Deleted

When the request is verified, the app owner will delete the user's account/profile data stored for the app.

Local Device Records

Some app records may be stored locally on the user's phone. Users can remove local data by deleting records inside the app or uninstalling the app from the device.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests should be processed within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact infotechvvd@gmail.com.
