Account Deletion

Feedit

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Home Privacy Policy Terms

Request deletion of your Feedit account and associated account-linked data using the email process below. You can start this request without installing or signing in to the app.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to:

infotechvvd@gmail.com

Use this subject:

Account deletion request - Feedit

Include the email address used to sign in to the app. If possible, also include your name so we can identify the correct account.

You can also start the email request inside Feedit at Settings > Privacy > Request account deletion. Do not send your password, app-lock PIN, or Password Manager master password.

What Data Is Deleted

After verifying the request, the app owner will delete the Feedit login account and associated profile, financial records, saved tool records, account cloud snapshots and account-linked Premium verification records held for the app, except information that must be retained for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. Any applicable exception will be explained when handling the request.

Sending the email starts a reviewed request; it is not instant automatic deletion. A separate email-request service for deleting selected cloud records while keeping the account active is not currently offered. In-app delete options remain available for supported individual records.

Local Device Records

Cloud-account deletion does not remotely erase copies already stored on your devices, exported files or files shared with others. Remove these copies separately. Signing out, clearing local app data or uninstalling does not itself delete the account's cloud copy. Password Manager vault files are device-local and are not part of financial/tool cloud snapshots.

Deletion markers and device recovery copies can be retained for synchronization integrity. Cloud-provider historical backups, if configured, follow their applicable retention settings; deleting the active account does not instantly rewrite historical backups.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests should be processed within 30 days.

Deleting a Feedit account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription or delete Google's payment records. Manage subscriptions separately in Google Play. You do not need to provide payment credentials to request account deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact infotechvvd@gmail.com.
